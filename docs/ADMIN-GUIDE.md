# Admin guide — managing the stock

This guide is for showroom staff. You don't need any technical knowledge.

**Dashboard address:** http://102.202.208.195/admin
You can also use the **Sign in** button at the top of the website, or the **Staff sign in** link at the bottom.

---

## Signing in and out

1. Open the dashboard and enter the email and password.
2. Tap **Show** to check the password while you type.
3. Click **Sign out** (top right) when you finish, especially on a shared computer.

- You stay signed in for 12 hours.
- After 5 wrong passwords, sign-in is blocked for 15 minutes on that device. Wait, then try again.
- If the website is restarted or updated, you need to sign in again. That's normal.

## Posting a car

1. Click **Post a car**.
2. **Photos.** Click *Add photos* or drag pictures in. You can add up to 20, as JPG, PNG or WebP. Big phone photos are resized automatically before they upload, so posting stays quick on mobile data.
   - The **first photo is the cover** that customers see on the card.
   - Drag photos to reorder them, or click **Make cover** on one.
   - Click **Remove** on a photo to take it off.
3. **The car.** Fill in:
   - **Make and model** (required), for example *Toyota RAV4*
   - **Year**: one year (*2021*) or a range (*2020-2022*)
   - **Body type**: pick SUV, Sedan, Pickup… or type your own. Body types become the filter buttons on the website.
   - **Condition**: New or Used
   - **Mileage** in km, and **Colour** (pick a swatch or type a name)
   - **Description**: engine, features, service history, papers
4. **Price and stock.**
   - **Price** in RWF (required)
   - **Units in stock**
   - **Starting price**: shows "From" before the price, for cars with several versions
   - **Feature this car**: puts it first on the website
   - **Show on website**: turn it off to save the car as a draft
5. Click **Publish**.

> **A car only appears on the website when it has at least one photo, is in stock, and is set to show on the website.**
> If you save a car without photos, the dashboard shows **No photos** and the car stays hidden until you add one.

## Everyday changes

| I want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Change price, photos, details | Click **Edit** on the car's row |
| Record a sale | Click **−** in the Stock column. At 0 it shows **Sold out** and leaves the website |
| Add stock back | Click **+**. The car shows again |
| Hide a car for now | Click its status to switch it from **Live** to **Hidden** |
| Remove a car for good | **Delete**, then confirm. Its photos are deleted too, and this can't be undone |
| Find a car | Use the search box, or the **All / Live / Hidden / sold** filter |

Changes show on the website straight away. Customers only need to refresh the page.

### What the status means

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Live** | On the website |
| **Hidden** | Turned off by you, so not on the website |
| **Sold out** | Stock is 0, so not on the website |
| **No photos** | Needs at least one photo before it can show |

The line under **Stock** shows: cars on the website, total units in stock, stock value (price × units), and cars that are hidden or sold out.

## Business details

Go to **Settings → Business details** to change:
- business name
- phone number
- WhatsApp number: country code first, numbers only, for example `250787615702`
- showroom address
- Google Maps link, used by the "Directions" button
- intro text under "Cars in stock"

Click **Save changes**.

## Changing the sign-in email or password

**Settings → Sign-in details**

1. Type a new email and/or a new password (at least 8 characters), twice.
2. Type your **current password** to confirm.
3. Click **Update sign-in details**.

Changing the password signs out every other device.

**Forgot the password?** Ask the person who maintains the website to reset it (see the Maintenance guide).

## Tips for good listings

- Shoot in daylight: front three-quarter, side, back, interior, dashboard, engine.
- Make the cover photo the best outside shot.
- Phone photos are fine at full size. The dashboard shrinks them to 1920 px before uploading.
- Put the important facts in the description: fuel or battery range, transmission, papers ready, warranty.
